HOW TO PLAY

THE FOOL / DURAK

THE TALON

Durak uses 36 cards, ranked 6 through Ace. Everyone receives six. The face-up bottom card sets trumps and is drawn last.

ATTACK

The lowest-trump holder begins. Later attacks must match any rank already visible in the battle. A round has at most six attacks and never more than the defender began with.

DEFEND

Cover an attack with a higher card of the same suit or any trump against a plain suit. Select your card, then tap the uncovered attack it should beat.

TAKE OR BEAT

If you cannot or do not want to defend, take the entire battle. Other players may throw in matching ranks before finishing the attack.

REFILL ORDER

The main attacker refills first, other attackers follow clockwise, and the defender draws last. Drawing stops permanently when the talon is empty.

THE FOOL

Once the talon is exhausted, players leave as their hands reach zero. The last player holding cards is the Durak.
