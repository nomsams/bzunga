HOW TO PLAY BAZUNGA

Remember your layout, improve it without revealing too much, and finish with the lowest score.

START & OBJECTIVE

Every player begins with four face-down layout cards and privately peeks at exactly two. Reduce your score; a black King scores −1.

YOUR TURN

Draw from the deck—or the discard pile in Rhino mode—then swap it into your layout, add it when required, or discard it face up.

SLAPPING

When the top discard matches a layout card, slap that layout card onto it. Only one successful slap is allowed per discard opportunity; wrong or duplicate slaps earn a penalty.

MAGIC CARDS

9 peeks at your own card, 10 secretly peeks at an opponent, Jack swaps blindly, Queen peeks then may swap, and a red King gives an opponent a penalty.

CALLING BAZUNGA

Call when you trust your score. Everyone else receives one final orbit turn, followed by a last reaction window for a legal slap.

MODES & WINNING

Rhino is the standard game and permits discard draws. Joker mode disables discard draws and uses its harder scoring rules. Lowest final score wins.
