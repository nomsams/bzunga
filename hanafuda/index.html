Hanafuda · Koi-Koi

Deal & turns

Two players receive 8 cards each; 8 cards form the field. Oya plays a hand card, captures the same month when possible, then automatically draws and resolves one deck card.

Matching choices

One match captures automatically. With two matching field cards, choose one. Three matching field cards stay stacked; the fourth captures all four.

Broken deals

Four of one month on the field triggers an immediate redeal. Teshi (four of one month) or Kuttsuki (four pairs) in a hand wins 6; simultaneous wins go to Oya.

Koi-Koi or Shobu

A new or upgraded Yaku lets you stop and score, or call Koi-Koi and continue. A final-turn Yaku must stop. If your opponent wins after your Koi-Koi, their score doubles.

Scoring

Base scores of 7+ double. The failed Koi-Koi penalty doubles again, multiplicatively. An exhausted round awards Oya 6 points and Oya remains dealer.

Core Yaku

Five/Four/Rainy Four/Three Brights; Boar-Deer-Butterflies; Poetry and Blue ribbons; 5+ Animals, 5+ Ribbons, and 10+ Chaff. Sake Cup counts as both Animal and Chaff.

Viewing setting

Moon Viewing and Blossom Viewing are off by default. Enable them as 5-point Yaku; the separate Lightning toggle can nullify them.

Full match

Play 3, 6 or 12 months. The highest total wins. The round winner becomes Oya; instant wins and Oya-ken retain the current Oya.
