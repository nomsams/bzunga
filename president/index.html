TABLE CONSTITUTION

How power changes hands

1. Build upward

Play a strictly higher rank than the pile and at least as many cards. A triple can beat a pair; an equal rank never can.

2. Twos are wild

A 2 has no value alone. Attach one or more 2s to a natural rank: K + 2 counts as two Kings. You cannot finish with a 2 in your final play. A fresh-pile leader holding only 2s is skipped; if every remaining player is blocked, the player with fewer cards ranks first and clockwise order after the blocked leader breaks ties.

3. Passing is final

Pass and you leave that pile. When only one active player remains, the pile clears and that player opens again.

4. Aces clear

Any valid Ace combination immediately discards the pile. The last player to play opens the next pile, unless they have finished.

5. First and later rounds

Cards are set aside when needed so every player receives exactly the same hand size. The 3♥ is always kept in play and starts before roles exist; later, the Slave starts.

6. The new order

First out becomes President; last becomes Slave. With five or more players, second and second-last become the two Vice roles.
